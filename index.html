    04 / STUDY BETTER

    Little habits. Big difference.

    Try these practical study techniques to build consistency.

    🎯

    Set one clear goal

    Choose a specific outcome before you start, like solving ten practice questions instead of simply “studying math.”

    🧠

    Test your memory

    Close the notes and explain what you learned in your own words. Practice recalling information, not only rereading it.

    🌿

    Take real breaks

    Stretch, walk or drink water during breaks so you return refreshed for your next focused study session.
